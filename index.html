What the assessments mean

Every verdict is derived from the thresholds set above, so the tool judges against targets set before the results came in.

All stages

Needs more data

4-day spend is below the minimum you set. Any verdict at this level would be noise, not signal — the ad hasn't been given enough budget to prove or disprove anything.

Keep

Passing every check for its layer's job. No action required.

TOF — judged on reach economics, never CPP

Add reach data

TOF cannot be judged without accounts reached. Import the 7-day CSV or enter reach manually.

Reach inefficient — switch off

Cost per 1,000 accounts reached is more than 30% above your band. The ad never bought audience efficiently, which is TOF's one job. Kill the concept, not just the asset.

Cheap reach, weak pull — rework creative

Buying reach under the band, but outbound CTR is below your floor. The audience sees it and nobody acts. That's a creative problem, not a delivery problem — the concept may survive with a new hook or tile.

Fatigued — replace creative, keep concept

Reach is efficient and CTR clears the floor, but frequency is above your ceiling, meaning it's increasingly re-showing to the same accounts. The ad worked and is now done. Retire the asset, reskin the concept.

TOM and MOF — judged on CPP against target

No purchases yet — watch

No result recorded, but spend is still under twice the target CPP. Too early to call — give it one more window.

No purchases — switch off

Spend has passed twice the target CPP with nothing to show. The ad has had target-level budget two times over and hasn't converted once.

Switch off

CPP is more than 40% above target. At that distance the ad is structurally unprofitable against your AOV, not just having a slow week.

Over target — watch

CPP is 10–40% above target: close enough that a good few days fixes it, far enough that it doesn't get a free pass. One more window before action.

On target but fatiguing

The economics are fine but frequency has passed your ceiling. Performance will decay from here — plan the creative refresh before the CPP tells you to.
