What the assessments mean

Every verdict comes from the blended CPP target and the bands set above. CPP tells you if an ad is working; reach, CTR and frequency tell you what kind of work it is doing and whether it is fatiguing.

Primary verdict — one per ad, all stages

Needs more data

4-day spend is below the minimum you set. Any verdict here would be noise, not signal.

No purchases yet — watch

No purchase recorded, but spend has not yet reached the stage's bad CPP line. Too early to call.

No purchases — switch off

Spend has passed the stage's bad CPP with nothing to show. It has had the budget and hasn't converted.

Switch off

CPP is at or above the stage's bad line (TOF 4×, TOM 3×, MOF 2× blended). Structurally unprofitable for its layer.

Over target — watch

CPP is above the stage's good line but below the bad line. One more window before action.

Keep

CPP is at or under the stage's good line. Doing its job. No action needed.

Exceptional

CPP beats the stage's good line by 25%+. At TOM and MOF that is the whole test. At TOF it also requires cheap reach (cost per 1,000 in the good band or better), so the ad is converting and doing genuine top-funnel work.

Movement flags — added alongside the verdict

Behaving like TOF

TOM only. A converting ad whose cost per 1,000 reached is in the TOF good band or cheaper — buying cheap broad reach while converting.

Behaving like MOF

TOM only. A converting ad with strong CPP but expensive, narrow reach (cost per 1,000 at or above the bad band) — closing warm people rather than buying audience.

Behaving like TOM

TOF only. CPP sits between TOM exceptional and TOF good while reach is at the average band or worse — converting like a mid-funnel ad rather than buying cheap top-funnel reach.

Diagnostic flags — added alongside the verdict

High cost/1000

TOF and TOM only. Cost per 1,000 accounts reached is at or above the bad band. The ad is buying reach expensively.

Low CTR

Outbound CTR is below the floor you set. People are seeing the ad but not clicking through.

High frequency

Frequency is above the stage ceiling (TOF base, TOM ×1.5, MOF ×2). The ad is re-showing to the same accounts.
